About Verbatra certificates Verbatra certificates are free and self-issued. The verification system reads from the browser's local storage on the device that generated the certificate, so it works for the recipient. For third-party verification, the recipient can show their downloaded PDF certificate which includes a unique ID. Verbatra does not currently maintain a server-side certificate registry; the device-local design is by intention to keep the courses fully private and free.
