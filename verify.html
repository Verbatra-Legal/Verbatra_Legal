Enter a certificate ID below to check whether it exists in this device's records. Verbatra certificates are self-issued and verifiable on the device that generated them.

About Verbatra certificates Verbatra certificates are self-issued. The verification system reads from the browser's local storage on the device that generated the certificate, so it works for the recipient. For third-party verification, the recipient can show their downloaded PDF certificate which includes a unique ID. Verbatra does not currently maintain a server-side certificate registry; the device-local design is by intention to keep the courses fully private.
